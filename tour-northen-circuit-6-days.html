6 Days Northern Circuit Classic

6 days, 5 nights

This is the safari we recommend for a first visit. In six days you see four very different landscapes, and each night is spent close to where the animals are.

  1. Arusha
  2. Tarangire
  3. Karatu
  4. Serengeti
  5. Ngorongoro
  6. Lake Manyara

Day by day

Choose your stay

Day 1

Arrive in Arusha

We meet you at Kilimanjaro or Arusha airport and transfer you to your hotel. Your guide visits in the evening to go through the route and answer questions.

Day 2

Tarangire National Park and Karatu

Drive about 2 to 2.5 hours to Tarangire. Spend the day on game drives among large elephant herds, giant baobabs and a wide range of birds, with a picnic lunch in the park. In the late afternoon drive on to Karatu.

Day 3

Karatu to the Serengeti

Cross the Ngorongoro Conservation Area and descend to the Serengeti plains. Expect around 5 to 6 hours on the road including game viewing, with a picnic lunch and an optional stop at Olduvai Gorge.

Stay

Day 4

Full day in the Serengeti

Set out at sunrise when the big cats are most active. Spend the day around the Seronera area looking for lions and leopards, with time for a long picnic lunch under an acacia. A dawn hot-air balloon flight can be added (extra cost, book ahead).

Stay

Day 5

Serengeti, Ngorongoro Crater and Karatu

After breakfast, drive up to the Ngorongoro highlands and down about 600 metres into the crater. The floor holds dense wildlife, with a chance of black rhino. Enjoy lunch by the hippo pool, then climb out and drive to Karatu.

Stay
Lodge in KaratuLuxury lodge in Karatu

Day 6

Lake Manyara and back to Arusha

A short morning drive to Lake Manyara National Park for forest, hippos, baboons and birdlife, plus the park's tree-climbing lions on a lucky day. After lunch, drive to Arusha (about 2 hours) for your airport transfer.
