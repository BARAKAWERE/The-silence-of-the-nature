7 Days Kilimanjaro, Marangu Route

7 days, 6 nights

The Marangu route sleeps in mountain huts rather than tents, and includes an extra day at Horombo Hut (3,720 m) for acclimatisation. Your guides check how you feel each day. Please speak to your doctor before booking a high-altitude trek.

  1. Marangu Gate
  2. Mandara Hut
  3. Horombo Hut
  4. Kibo Hut
  5. Uhuru Peak

Day 2

Marangu Gate to Mandara Hut

Register at the gate (about 1,860 m) and walk through montane forest to Mandara Hut at about 2,720 m. Expect roughly 5 hours of easy walking and a chance to see colobus monkeys.

Mandara to Horombo Hut

Day 5

Horombo to Kibo Hut

Cross the high desert known as the saddle to Kibo Hut at about 4,720 m. Eat early and rest, because the summit climb starts around midnight.

Day 6

Summit day: Uhuru Peak and down to Horombo

Start around midnight by headlamp, climb to Gilman's Point and continue along the crater rim to Uhuru Peak at 5,895 m, for sunrise at the top. Descend to Kibo for a meal and rest, then continue to Horombo. A very long day, often 14 hours or more.
