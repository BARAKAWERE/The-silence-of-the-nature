3 Days Birding and Photography

3 days, 2 nights

We take fewer drives and stay longer at each stop, so you have time to watch behaviour and wait for the shot. Which species you see depends on the month; your guide tells you what to look for each morning. This is a sample plan and can be extended or combined with a longer safari.

  1. Arusha
  2. Tarangire
  3. Lake Manyara
  4. Karatu

Day by day

Choose your stay

Day 1

Arusha to Tarangire

Drive to Tarangire (about 2 to 2.5 hours). Spend the afternoon on a slow drive along the river and swamps, where storks, raptors and many smaller birds gather, plus elephants for variety.

Day 2

Tarangire sunrise and Lake Manyara

Leave at first light for the best light and activity. Return for breakfast, then drive to Lake Manyara for the afternoon: forest birds, water birds along the shore, hippos and baboons. Overnight in Karatu.

Day 3

Karatu birding and return

Start with a morning walk around Karatu's farmland and forest edge for species you do not see in the parks. Then drive to Arusha (about 3 hours) for your airport transfer.
