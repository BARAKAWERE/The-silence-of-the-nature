1 Day Culture and Heritage

1 day

Meet the people who live beside the parks. The day is slow and on foot, with time to talk and to buy work directly from the artists. The plan below is an example, and we adjust it to the season and to who is available.

  1. Karatu or Arusha
  2. Mto wa Mbu
  3. Art workshop

Your day

The day

Village walk, lunch and art workshop

Morning: drive to Mto wa Mbu for a guided walk through its market and banana plantations, meeting farmers and traders. Midday: lunch cooked by a local family. Afternoon: visit an art workshop where painters show their methods and sell their work. A visit to a community-run Maasai boma can be added, with fees going to the community. Return to your lodge in the late afternoon.
