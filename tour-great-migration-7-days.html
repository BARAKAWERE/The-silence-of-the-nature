7 days, 6 nights

Where the herds are, month by month

January to MarchSouthern Serengeti and the Ndutu area. Calving season, with large herds and many predators.
April to JuneCentral Serengeti, then the Western Corridor. River crossings at the Grumeti are possible around May and June.
July to OctoberNorthern Serengeti and the Mara River, where crossings happen.
November and DecemberThe herds move back south towards the short rains.

Movements follow rain, so these are patterns, not promises. We confirm the best area when you book.
