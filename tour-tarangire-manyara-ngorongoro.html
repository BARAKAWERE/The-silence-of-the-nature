4 Days Tarangire, Manyara and Ngorongoro

4 days, 3 nights

Choose this if you have limited time or prefer shorter drives. You still see elephants, big cats, birds and the Ngorongoro Crater.

  1. Arusha
  2. Tarangire
  3. Lake Manyara
  4. Karatu

Choose your stay

Day 1

Arusha to Tarangire

We pick you up in Arusha and drive about 2 to 2.5 hours to Tarangire. Spend the afternoon on a game drive among elephant herds and baobabs.
